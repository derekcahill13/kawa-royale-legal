Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 8, 2026

Kawa Royale ("Kawa Royale", "we", "our", "us", "the App") is a free-to-play social casino game published by Derek Cahill. The App is rated 17+ and is not intended for users under 17. This Privacy Policy describes the categories of information the App collects, how that information is used, who it is shared with, and the choices you have.

The App uses virtual currency only. No real money is wagered or won, and virtual coins cannot be redeemed for real money or anything of monetary value.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information stored locally on your device

The following data is stored in your device's local storage by default and is not transmitted to our servers unless you sign in for cloud sync (see §1.2):

  • Virtual coin balance, game progress, level, XP, and per-machine statistics
  • Daily bonus and login streak status
  • Charm collection and album progress
  • Sound, haptic, and notification preferences
  • Tutorial and onboarding completion state
  • Game Pass and Battle Pass entitlement status

1.2 Authentication data

The App supports the following sign-in methods:

  • Sign in with Apple — primary authentication method on iOS. We receive an Apple-managed user identifier and, if you choose to share it, your email address. We do not receive your real name unless you choose to share it. Apple's "Hide My Email" relay is fully supported.
  • Anonymous authentication — if you don't sign in with Apple, the App provisions an anonymous Firebase account so cloud sync can still work. This generates a random opaque identifier with no personal information attached.
  • Google sign-in — available as a fallback on platforms where Sign in with Apple is unavailable. If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account email and display name.

1.3 Cloud-synced game data

When you sign in (Apple, Anonymous, or Google), the following game data is synced to our backend (Google Firebase Firestore) so you can play across devices and so social features work:

  • Your Firebase user identifier (UID)
  • Virtual coin balance and game progression
  • Display name and avatar selection (visible to other players — see §1.7)
  • Charm and theme inventory
  • Friends list, gifts sent and received, club memberships
  • Leaderboard rank and recent big-win events
  • Active limited-time offers and personalized promotional eligibility

1.4 Purchase data

In-app purchases are processed by Apple via StoreKit. Apple, not Kawa Royale, handles your payment information. After a successful purchase, the App sends Apple's signed receipt to our server-side validator (a Firebase Cloud Function) to confirm the transaction is legitimate before granting virtual goods. The receipt contains the product ID, transaction ID, and purchase timestamp; it does not contain your name, email, or payment method.

1.5 Diagnostic and crash data

The App uses Firebase Crashlytics to collect anonymous crash reports and diagnostic data when the App crashes or encounters a fatal error. This includes:

  • Device model, operating system version, and app version
  • Stack trace at the point of failure
  • Coarse system state (memory, orientation) at the time of the crash

Crashlytics data is anonymous and cannot be linked back to you personally. We use it strictly to identify and fix bugs.

1.6 Analytics data

The App uses Firebase Analytics to understand aggregate usage patterns (which features are used, where players drop off, how long sessions last). Analytics events are pseudonymous — they are tied to a randomized installation identifier, not to your real-world identity. We do not use this data to build advertising profiles, and the App does not include third-party advertising SDKs.

1.7 Public-profile data (visible to other players)

The following information is visible to other players inside the App when you participate in social features:

  • Your display name (chosen by you)
  • Your selected avatar
  • Your level and recent big wins (in the Big Win Feed and friend leaderboards)
  • Your club membership status (if you join a club)

Your virtual coin balance, IAP history, email, and Apple/Google account identifiers are never shown to other players.

1.8 Push notification tokens

If you grant notification permission, the App registers an Apple Push Notification (APNS) device token with our backend so we can send time-sensitive alerts (daily bonus reminders, limited-time offers, gift received notifications). The APNS token is opaque and is not personally identifying. You can revoke notification permission at any time in iOS Settings.

2. How We Use Information

  • Operate the game: persist your progress, validate purchases, run social features (gifts, clubs, friends, leaderboards), credit daily bonuses
  • Improve the game: aggregate analytics tells us which features players enjoy, which they ignore, and where the difficulty curve breaks
  • Detect and prevent abuse: server-side receipt validation, anti-cheat economy ledger, anti-fraud monitoring on club pools and gift transfers
  • Diagnose problems: Crashlytics tells us when the App is crashing so we can ship fixes
  • Send notifications: APNS push alerts you've explicitly opted into

3. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your real-world name, address, phone number, or government identifiers.
  • We do not collect your contact list, photos, microphone audio, camera image, location, or browsing history.
  • We do not include third-party advertising SDKs (no AdMob, Facebook Audience Network, IronSource, AppLovin, Unity Ads, etc.).
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.
  • We do not use your data to build advertising profiles or to target you with ads outside the App.

9. Data Security

Cloud-synced data is stored in Google Firebase with TLS encryption in transit and at rest. Firestore security rules restrict each user's data so only that authenticated user (or our server-side Cloud Functions) can read or write it. Local data is stored in your device's standard storage and is protected by iOS device security. No system is perfectly secure; if we detect a data breach affecting you, we will notify you as required by applicable law.
