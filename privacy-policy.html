Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 16, 2026

Kawa Royale ("we", "our", "the app") is a free-to-play social casino game. This policy describes how we handle information when you use the app.

1. Information We Collect

Game Data (stored locally on your device):

  • Virtual coin balance, game progress, level, and XP
  • Daily bonus and streak status
  • Game Pass ownership status
  • Sound and notification preferences

This data is stored in your device's local storage and is not transmitted to our servers unless you opt in to cloud save.

Cloud Save (optional, requires sign-in):

  • If you sign in with Google or use anonymous authentication via Firebase, we store your game progress in Firebase Firestore so you can sync across devices.
  • Firebase Authentication may collect your Google account email and display name if you sign in with Google.
  • Anonymous authentication creates a random identifier with no personally identifiable information.

Purchase Data:

  • In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store using StoreKit. We do not collect or store your payment information. Purchase receipts are validated on-device.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email, phone number, or address (unless you opt in to Google sign-in for cloud save)
  • We do not use advertising SDKs or ad tracking
  • We do not sell, share, or rent any user data to third parties
  • We do not use analytics or tracking SDKs

3. Third-Party Services

The app may use the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Authentication & Firestore (Google) — for optional cloud save. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Apple StoreKit — for processing in-app purchases (on-device, no data sent to third parties). Apple Privacy Policy

4. Data Storage & Security

Game data is stored locally on your device using standard browser/WebView local storage. If you use cloud save, data is stored in Google Firebase Firestore with standard security rules that restrict access to authenticated users only.

5. Children's Privacy

Kawa Royale is rated 12+ and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app contains simulated gambling mechanics using virtual currency only — no real money is wagered or won.

6. Data Deletion

You can delete all local data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. If you have used cloud save, you can request deletion of your cloud data by contacting us at the email below.
